# RQ4 decision baseline and success criteria

Written on 31 October 2026, before any RQ4 outcome was computed. The yearly sales tables had
already been seen during RQ1 to RQ3, so this is not a blind test, but no back-test result had been.

## Status of the baseline: a stand-in

The proposal calls for a *documented* account of how the distributor chooses where and what to grow.
That account has not been obtained yet. Until it is, the baseline is a stated rule:

> **Grow where we already sell most:** back the territory-category pairs with the biggest current sales.

This is an assumption about intuition-based decisions, not evidence of what this distributor does.
The thesis must say so. To replace it, ask the distributor's management:

1. When you last pushed a product category harder in a territory, how did you choose them?
2. What information did you look at, and who made the call?
3. Which territories or categories would you back next year, and why?

## The test

- **Decision point:** the end of FY2023-24. Every score is computed from a copy of the data cut off
  at that year, so later sales cannot influence it.
- **Pool:** all active territory x category pairs, leaving out the hub, as in RQ3.
- **Picks:** the top 15 for each method, the same number as the RQ3 shortlist.
- **Outcome (a "hit"):** the pair's sales in FY2025-26 beat what they would have been had they moved
  with the whole pool. Measuring against the pool matters because the business was shrinking.

## Success criteria

1. The system's top 15 has a higher hit rate than the baseline's top 15.
2. The system's hit rate also beats random picks from the pairs eligible for recommendation
   (one-sided permutation test, p < 0.05). This asks whether the score adds anything beyond
   finding a gap, since low-selling pairs tend to rebound anyway (regression to the mean).

**Pass:** both. **Partial:** criterion 1 only. **Fail:** criterion 1 not met.

Reported but not used for the verdict: one-year outcomes, a second decision point (FY2024-25),
rupee measures, a room-to-grow-only ranking, and what happened to territories flagged by RQ1.
The scoring points are not changed after seeing these results.

## Known limits

- **Nobody acted on these recommendations.** The test asks whether pairs the system would have picked
  did better on their own, not what would have happened if the distributor had pushed them.
- **Few picks and few decision points:** 15 picks from one distributor, so low statistical power.
  Pairs in the same territory also tend to move together.

### Forward test on April to October 2026 (main evidence, because the outcomes were unknown when the picks were made)
- **Picks:** the RQ3 top 15 from FY2025-26, as shown on the Expansion Opportunities page since 1 November 2026,
  before the new data arrived. Baseline: the top 15 pairs by FY2025-26 sales. Also room-to-grow only and random picks.
- **Outcome:** each pair's sales from April 2026 to the latest month received, against the same months a year
  earlier. A hit beats the pool's movement over those months.
- **Criteria and verdict:** the same as above (Pass, Partial or Fail). The back-test verdict (Partial) stands as written.
- **Limits:** seven months, one decision point, and the same 15-pick sample size.
